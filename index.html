Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version BillBook does not collect, store or sell your data. Everything you enter (parties, bills and stock) is saved on your phone. If you sign in with Google, a private backup is kept in your own Google Drive, so you can restore it after reinstalling. We have no servers and we cannot see your data.
